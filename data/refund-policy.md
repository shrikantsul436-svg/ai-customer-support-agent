# E-Commerce Refund Policy

## Rule 1 — Refund Window

A refund request must be submitted within 30 days
of the order's delivery date.

Requests submitted after 30 days must be denied.

## Rule 2 — Eligible Reasons

The following reasons are eligible:

- Damaged product
- Defective product
- Wrong product received
- Product significantly different from description

## Rule 3 — Non-Eligible Reasons

The following reasons are not eligible:

- Customer changed their mind
- Customer no longer wants the product
- Normal wear and tear
- Customer caused the damage

## Rule 4 — Digital Products

Digital products are non-refundable after the
customer has downloaded the product.

## Rule 5 — Previous Refund

An order that already has a completed refund
cannot receive another refund.

## Rule 6 — Refund Amount

The refund cannot exceed the original amount
paid for the order.

## Rule 7 — Shipping

Shipping charges are refundable only when the
seller is responsible for the issue.

## Rule 8 — Required Validation

Before approving a refund, the support agent
must verify:

1. Customer
2. Order
3. Delivery date
4. Refund history
5. Refund reason
6. Product type
7. Applicable policy rules

The AI agent must not approve a refund without
completing these checks.
